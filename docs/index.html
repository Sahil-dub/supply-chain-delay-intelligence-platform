ENGINEERING STORY

What a recruiter can inspect

  • ETL: validation, cleaning, transformation and analytics-mart generation.
  • SQL/PostgreSQL: relational structures, KPI views and business-focused analytics.
  • API: typed FastAPI endpoints for KPIs, trends, supplier, warehouse, inventory and high-risk shipment analytics.
  • ML: preprocessing pipelines, historical features, evaluation, feature importance and serialized inference.
  • Quality: pytest, Ruff, reproducible seed-based synthetic data and Dockerized PostgreSQL.
  • BI: Power BI dashboard planning and analytics-ready outputs.
